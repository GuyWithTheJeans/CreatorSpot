HOW TO INSTALL CREATORSPOT
==========================

Easiest way: double-click "Install CreatorSpot" in this folder.
A small window opens and walks you through it.

Doing it by hand (Brave):

1. Open Brave. Type  brave://extensions  in the address bar and press Enter.
   (Chrome: chrome://extensions   Edge: edge://extensions)

2. Turn ON the "Developer mode" switch in the top-right corner.

3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose THIS folder (the CreatorSpot folder
   on your Desktop). Click "Select Folder".

4. Done! Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin CreatorSpot.

Important: keep this folder where it is. If you move or delete it,
the extension stops working.

To share with a friend: right-click this folder > "Send to" >
"Compressed (zipped) folder", send them the ZIP, and tell them to unzip it
to their Desktop first.
