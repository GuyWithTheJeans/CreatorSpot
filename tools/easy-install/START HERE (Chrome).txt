CREATORSPOT FOR GOOGLE CHROME
=============================

Takes about 1 minute.

1. Make sure you UNZIPPED this first.
   Right-click the ZIP file > "Extract All..." > choose your Desktop > "Extract".
   You should now have a "CreatorSpot" folder on your Desktop. Open it.
   (If you just double-clicked the ZIP to look inside, it will not work.)

2. Double-click "Install CreatorSpot". A small window opens and walks you
   through it. If Windows asks "Do you want to run this file?", click "Run"
   (or "More info" > "Run anyway").

   Or do it by hand:
   a. Open Chrome. Type  chrome://extensions  in the address bar, press Enter.
   b. Turn ON "Developer mode" (switch in the top-right corner).
   c. Click "Load unpacked", pick the CreatorSpot folder on your Desktop,
      click "Select Folder".

3. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top-right of Chrome and click the pin
   next to CreatorSpot.

Done. Visit a site with ads (for example ign.com) and you will see
GuyWithTheJeans and iamDoughnutDude promos where the ads were.

Keep the CreatorSpot folder on your Desktop. If you move or delete it,
the extension stops working.

GETTING A NEW VERSION
When you get a newer ZIP: delete the old CreatorSpot folder, unzip the new
one to the same place, then go to chrome://extensions and click the
circular-arrow (reload) button on CreatorSpot.
