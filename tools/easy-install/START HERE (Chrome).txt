CREATORSPOT: HOW TO SET IT UP
=============================

About 5 minutes. Works in Chrome, Brave and Edge.


PART 1: INSTALL IT
------------------

1. UNZIP FIRST.
   Right-click the ZIP file > "Extract All..." > choose your Desktop > "Extract".
   You now have a "CreatorSpot" folder on your Desktop. Open it.
   (If you just double-clicked the ZIP to look inside, it will not work.)

   Already had an older CreatorSpot? Delete the old CreatorSpot folder from your
   Desktop first, then unzip this one in the same place.

2. Open Chrome. Click the address bar at the top, type  chrome://extensions
   and press Enter.

3. Turn ON "Developer mode" (the switch in the top-right corner of that page).

4. If CreatorSpot is ALREADY in the list: click its circular-arrow (reload)
   button. Done with Part 1.

   If it is NOT in the list: click "Load unpacked" (top-left), pick the
   CreatorSpot folder on your Desktop, and click "Select Folder".

5. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top-right of Chrome, then click the pin
   next to CreatorSpot so its icon stays in your toolbar.


PART 2: SIGN IN SO YOUR PROFILE SHOWS UP FOR EVERYONE
-----------------------------------------------------

6. Click the CreatorSpot icon in your toolbar, then "Open dashboard".

7. On the left, click "Creators".

8. Click the purple "Sign in with Twitch" button.
   A Twitch window pops up. Log in if it asks, then click "Authorize".

9. You should now see "Signed in as <your name>". Your Twitch picture and
   channel link are filled in automatically.


PART 3: CHANGE YOUR PROFILE AND PROMOS
--------------------------------------

10. Still on "Creators": find YOUR card and click "Edit my profile".
    Change your name, bio, color or picture, and add more links (YouTube,
    TikTok, Kick, Discord, website). Click "Save changes".

11. Your promos: on the left click "Campaigns". Your campaigns have an "Edit"
    button. Change the headline, message, style, picture or video, and click
    "Save campaign". To make a new one, click "New campaign".

Everything you save shows up on EVERYONE's CreatorSpot within a few minutes.
Want to see others' changes right away? Creators > "Sync now".


GOOD TO KNOW
------------

- Keep the CreatorSpot folder on your Desktop. Deleting it removes the extension.
- Other creators' cards say "Synced from CreatorSpot". Only they can change
  their own profile.
- When there is a new version, the CreatorSpot popup tells you. Double-click
  "Update CreatorSpot" in the CreatorSpot folder, then click the reload arrow
  on CreatorSpot in chrome://extensions.
