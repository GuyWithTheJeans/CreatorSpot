CreatorSpot finds standard ad slots on the websites you visit and shows promotions for independent streamers and creators instead.

Your channel

Are you a creator?

Add your channel and CreatorSpot can promote you too. You can add more platforms, pictures and campaigns later in the dashboard.

Not a creator? Skip this step and simply enjoy discovering creators.

Rotation

Who should appear in promotions?

You can change this at any time from the toolbar button.

All set

You’re ready to go.

Browse as usual. When a page has a standard ad slot, a creator promotion appears in its place, clearly labeled “Creator promo”.

Pin CreatorSpot

Click the puzzle-piece icon in your toolbar and pin CreatorSpot to switch modes or pause it on a site in one click.

Using Brave?

Brave Shields hide ad slots on their own. CreatorSpot detects this and places promotions beside hidden slots. If a site still shows no promotions, lower Shields for that site.
