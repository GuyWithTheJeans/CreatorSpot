CreatorSpot

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version

CreatorSpot works inside your browser and never records or sends what you browse. Creators can choose to sign in with Twitch so their public creator profile is shown to everyone who uses CreatorSpot. Viewers never need an account. We never sell, share or rent information about you or your browsing.

Creator accounts (optional)

  • Signing in with Twitch gives CreatorSpot your Twitch user name, display name and profile picture. Twitch also shares your email address with our sign-in provider (Supabase). It is used only for the login and is never shown or shared.
  • What you put in your creator profile (name, bio, color, channel links, campaigns, and pictures or videos you upload) is public: every CreatorSpot user downloads it so your promotions can appear for them. Do not put private information in it.
  • You can sign out at any time. To delete your profile completely, contact the CreatorSpot team.

What CreatorSpot reads

  • Web pages you visit. To find standard ad slots, CreatorSpot looks at the structure of each page inside your browser. Page contents are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • The website address of the current tab. This is used only to respect sites you have paused and is never recorded.

What CreatorSpot stores, on your device only

  • Your settings, creator profiles, campaigns and any images or videos you upload.
  • Promotion statistics: the number of times each campaign was displayed and clicked, per day, for the last 90 days. No website addresses, page titles or browsing history are stored.
  • Rotation state, so placements stay fair between creators.

You can export, import or delete all of this from the dashboard at any time. Uninstalling CreatorSpot removes it completely.

Network requests

  • Every few minutes CreatorSpot downloads the public creator profiles (and their pictures) from its database at supabase.co, and pictures from Twitch's image server. It stores them in your browser, so the web pages you visit never contact CreatorSpot's servers. Every hour it asks GitHub whether a newer version of CreatorSpot exists. These downloads send no cookies and nothing about you or the pages you visit.
  • If you sign in as a creator, saving your profile sends it to the CreatorSpot database.
  • If you enable the optional Twitch live-status integration, CreatorSpot asks Twitch whether the creators in your rotation are live. These requests go directly from your browser to api.twitch.tv, using credentials you provide, and only after you grant permission.
  • When you click a promotion, your browser opens the creator’s channel. That site’s own privacy policy applies.

Ad blocking and honesty

CreatorSpot never generates fake ad impressions or clicks. When it replaces an ad slot, the original ad is hidden, so the ad network’s own visibility measurement records it as not viewed. If you turn on “Stop ad networks from loading”, requests to well-known ad networks are blocked entirely and those ads are never downloaded. CreatorSpot’s own statistics only count promotions that were actually visible on screen.

Permissions

  • Read and change site data: required to find ad slots and display promotions in them.
  • Storage and unlimited storage: keeps your settings and uploaded media on this device.
  • Block content (declarativeNetRequest): stops known ad networks from loading, only if you turn that option on.
  • api.twitch.tv (optional): only requested if you set up live status.

Children

CreatorSpot does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the extension and the date above will change. Questions can be sent through the support link on the CreatorSpot store listing.
